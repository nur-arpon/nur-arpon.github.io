Process

Five steps, no surprises.

You always know what is happening, what comes next and what it will cost.

  1. Brief

    You tell me what you need, in your own words. The brief builder below makes it quick.

  2. Plan

    I reply with a plan and a quote: what gets built, in what order, and by when.

  3. Build

    Small steps you can see. You get real builds to try, not just progress reports.

  4. Launch

    Store listing, website, privacy policy and search setup, shipped properly.

  5. Support

    Launch is not goodbye. Fixes and updates after launch are part of the plan we agree.

Questions

        
Asked before you ask.

        
Something else on your mind? Put it in your brief.

      

      

        
How much does it cost? 
Every project is different, so there is no price list. Tell me about yours and I'll reply with a plan and a quote before any work starts.

        
Do you work with clients in other countries? 
Yes. SpaceZ works remotely with clients anywhere in the world. Most of the work happens in writing, and calls are set for a time that suits you.

        
Who will actually build it? 
Me, Nur Ifran Arpon, with the student developers I build with: the same people who made Spaceadom, Spike and FileZ.

        
Can you publish my app on the Microsoft Store? 
Yes. SpaceZ has published three apps there: Spaceadom, Spike and FileZ. That covers the listing, the screenshots, the privacy policy and the submission itself.

        
Do you build for Mac or iPhone? 
We are now taking on macOS: Spaceadom's Mac beta is out, and so is a Linux beta. For iPhone, ask: you will get an honest answer about whether we are the right fit.

        
What if I only have a rough idea? 
That is the normal place to start. Describe the problem you want solved, and the plan will turn it into something concrete.
