The idea

Your Downloads folder, tidy by itself. Nothing is ever deleted, and every move has a Put back button.

01 · Patient

It waits for downloads to finish.

Pictures and documents move two minutes after they stop changing; installers and zips after five.

02 · Reversible

Put back, always.

Every move can be undone, plus one button to put everything from today back.

How it is built

Private by design
Smart

Knows where a file came from

Files from one website can go to their own folder automatically.

Private

No network code at all

No AI, no account and no internet. Your files never leave your PC.

Native

Rust and Tauri

Small, fast and light on memory, published on the Microsoft Store.
