Privacy

Right now, nothing.

This page is about this website, nur-arpon.github.io. Each SpaceZ app has its own privacy policy, linked from its own page.

Last updated 5 October 2026

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no cookies and no sign-in. The fonts and pictures are served from this website itself, not from other companies.

Who hosts it

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical details every browser sends when it loads a page, such as your IP address. SpaceZ does not see or keep those. GitHub's own privacy statement explains how it handles them.

The brief builder

The "Start a project" form on the Hire page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to this website or stored anywhere. At the end it puts them into an email in your own email app, and nothing is sent until you press Send there. If you copy the brief, it goes to your clipboard only.

Things your browser remembers

If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers that choice on your own device (in local storage) so the site looks the same next time. It never leaves your device and you can clear it with your browser's site data.

Links to other sites

Links to the Microsoft Store, GitHub, Ko-fi and the product websites take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

What may change

Visitor analytics (counting visits to pages) may be added to this website later. If that happens, this page will say so first: which service is used, what it counts and how to opt out.

Questions

Write to Nur Ifran Arpon at the email address shown on the Hire page, or through GitHub.
