Privacy

Right now, nothing.

This page is about this website, nur-arpon.github.io. Each of my apps has its own privacy policy, linked from its own page. Last updated 13 October 2026.

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no cookies and no sign-in. The fonts, pictures and scripts are served from this website itself, not from other companies.

Who hosts it

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical details every browser sends when it loads a page, such as your IP address. I do not see or keep those. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles them.

Getting in touch

The email and WhatsApp buttons only open your own email or WhatsApp app. Nothing is sent until you press Send there, and this website never sees what you write.

Things your browser remembers

If you switch between the Black and Navy looks, your browser remembers that choice on your own device (in local storage). It never leaves your device, and you can clear it with your browser's site data.

Links to other sites

Links to the Microsoft Store, GitHub, Ko-fi, WhatsApp and my project websites take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

What may change

If visitor counting is ever added, this page will say so first: which service is used, what it counts and how to opt out.
