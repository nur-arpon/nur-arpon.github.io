Windows · free on the Microsoft Store

Spaceadom

Hold Space, tap an app's initial, and it opens. Tap it again and it's gone. Tap Space on its own and it types a space, always.

  • A chord, not a search box. Space + B is your browser, every time, with nothing to type and no list to read.
  • A ring of your apps under the mouse: hold the middle button, drift towards one, let go.
  • Touchpad edges become sliders for brightness, volume and video.
  • Native and light: a Rust core with a system-wide keyboard hook and a Tauri v2 dashboard. Windows 10 and 11, x64 and ARM64.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics in the app.
On the Microsoft Store macOS: beta Linux: beta
Get it free Try it in your browser

Where it started: SpaceToggle OS, an AutoHotkey script, tested with two friends and published on 26 May 2026. Spaceadom is its native rewrite.

Windows, Android and a robot

Spike + Desk Buddy

Spike is a desk robot dog with a face, a memory and a personality. While his body is being built, the same character lives on Windows and Android.

  • On the Microsoft Store for Windows, and a download for Android.
  • His brain runs on your own computer: wake words, memory, alarms and reminders. Conversation goes through Google Gemini with your own free key.
  • Desk Buddy, the body: ESP32-S3 boards, a 4.3-inch touch-screen face, thirteen micro servos and a fully 3D-printed shell, modelled in SolidWorks from Python scripts.
  • Honest status: the robot is a prototype being built now. Walking and give-paw are still in development.
App on the Microsoft Store Robot: prototype, building now

FileZ

Your Downloads folder, tidy by itself. Nothing is ever deleted, and every move has a Put back button.

  • Waits for downloads to finish. Pictures and documents move two minutes after they stop changing, installers and zips after five.
  • Knows where a file came from, so files from one website can go to their own folder.
  • Put back on every move, plus "Put everything from today back".
  • No AI, no account, no internet. FileZ has no network code at all.
On the Microsoft Store RustTauri v2

Android · Flutter and Firebase

Mistri Lagbe

"Mistri lagbe?" is Bangla for "need a tradesperson?" The app and its website let people book local mistris for electrical, plumbing, gas, AC, paint, furniture, CCTV and lift work.

  • Bangla first, with English one tap away.
  • Book from the app or straight from the website; bookings land in Firebase.
  • Phone first, with a proper desktop layout for the web.
  • Status: in testing on Android, not on Google Play yet.
