The idea

A desk robot dog with a face, a memory and a personality. While his body is being built, the same character already lives on Windows and Android.

01 · The body

Designed like a product.

Every board, servo and wire hidden inside a shell modelled part by part in SolidWorks, driven from Python scripts.

02 · From the side

Built to move.

Micro servos for the legs and head, a touch-screen face, sensors and a rechargeable battery pack.

03 · The app

The same character, today.

Wake words, memory, alarms and reminders, with his brain on your own computer and conversation through Google Gemini.

What is inside

Honest status: prototype
Brain

ESP32-S3 boards

A 4.3-inch touch-screen board is the face and the brain, talking to servo drivers and sensors.

Power

Two 18650 cells

A protected battery pack, a fuse and a switch, and 5-volt converters that feed the servos and boards.

Status

Being built now

The app is out. The robot is a prototype on the bench; walking and give-paw are still in development.
